A few general tips:

- **The Minister matters.** A Minister with a strong perk, like Paul's EZPZ or Cole's Mining Fiesta, can move prices almost as much as a Mayor.
- **Elections are visible early.** Most of the price movement happens once it's clear who will win, not on the day they take office. Buy-before positions should be in while the election is still close.
- **Big orders move prices.** At your scale, large buys and sells will shift the price themselves, especially on thinner markets like Chimera or Necron's Handles. Splitting orders across several days helps.
